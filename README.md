# CORUM public board

A snapshot of the CORUM influence map and research library, taken on 19 Nov 2026.

- `index.html`: pick a macro shock and see which industries it reaches.
- `map.html`: every driver, industry and link, with the reason and the evidence grade for each link.
- `companies.html`, `t/`: one page per company and fund: where it sits on the map, what would move it, a dated analysis.
- `ecosystems.html`, `reports.html`: network graphs and research reports.
- `how.html`: what the evidence grades mean.

Research notes produced with AI assistance. Not investment advice. May be wrong or out of date. Shock numbers are relative scores from the map, not forecasts of returns.

This repository holds the built site only. It is generated; edits made here are overwritten by the next build.
