1. Executive Summary & Investment Thesis

1.1 The Physical Chokepoint of Living Medicines

While clinical-stage biotechnology investors face binary Phase 2/3 efficacy readouts, FDA clinical holds, and dilutive equity financings, CryoPort, Inc. (CYRX) operates as the indispensable physical toll collector of the global Cell and Gene Therapy (CGT) ecosystem. Autologous CAR-T therapies, tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs), gene-edited hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs), and allogeneic off-the-shelf cellular medicines are living biological constructs. If a cryogenic dry-vapor shipper warms above the glass-transition temperature of water ($-135^\circ\text{C}$) during transit between an apheresis hospital, a central CDMO manufacturing facility, and the patient's bedside, intracellular ice recrystallization destroys the living dose—resulting in an irreplaceable $450,000 to $3,500,000 therapeutic loss and potentially fatal patient delay.

Cryoport commands ~70% global market share in specialized CGT cryogenic logistics and >65% global manufacturing share in cryogenic aluminum dewars and stainless-steel freezers (via its subsidiary MVE Biological Solutions). Today, CYRX supports 779+ active global CGT clinical trials (including 87 Phase 3 trials) and 22 FDA/EMA-approved commercial therapies.

1.2 Why CYRX Is Mispriced Today (~3.5x EV/Sales vs. 6x–10x Historical Range)

Between 2022 and 2024, CYRX suffered a severe multiple compression (falling from >$60/share to single digits) driven by two transient headwinds: (1) a post-COVID global freeze in laboratory cryogenic freezer orders at MVE Biological Solutions, and (2) fears that CYRX's convertible senior notes due November 2026 would force dilutive distress financing.

Both overhangs were structurally eliminated in mid-2025, yet the equity still trades at an depressed ~3.5x 2026E EV/Sales (~$688M Enterprise Value on ~$195M continuing revenue):

  1. The June 2025 CRYOPDP Sale to DHL (~$200M Cash + Global Strategic Alliance): CYRX divested 100% of its lower-margin, asset-heavy courier subsidiary CRYOPDP to DHL Group (DHL) for ~$200M in gross cash (~3.5x MOIC on CYRX's €49M acquisition price in 2020) while forming a long-term strategic alliance with DHL Health Logistics.
  2. Fortress Balance Sheet (+$164.7M Net Cash): With $396.7M in cash and short-term investments against $232.0M in total debt, CYRX holds +$164.7M in net cash (~19.3% of market cap). Management can retire the remaining ~$186M of 0.75% November 2026 convertible notes entirely out of cash while still retaining >$190M in unencumbered liquidity.
  3. The Commercial BLA Escalator (+29% YoY): Continuing-operations revenue rose +12% YoY to $176.2M in FY2025, led by Commercial Cell & Gene Therapy revenue surging +29% YoY to a record $35.9M (with Q4 2025 commercial revenue up +34% YoY) as earlier-line label expansions for Carvykti (LEGN / JNJ), Yescarta (GILD), Breyanzi (BMY), Casgevy (VRTX / CRSP), and Amtagvi (IOVA) ramped globally.
  4. Positive Adjusted EBITDA Inflection: Gross margins expanded 230 bps to 47.1% in FY2025, narrowing Adjusted EBITDA loss from $(14.6)M in FY2024 to $(3.0)M in FY2025 and putting CYRX on track for sustained positive Adjusted EBITDA in FY2026 ($10M–$14M).

Why FDA BLA Lock-In Prevents Price Competition

Unlike standard pharmaceutical cold-chain freight ($2^\circ\text{C}$ to $8^\circ\text{C}$ refrigerated pallets), an autologous or allogeneic cell therapy requires Chain-of-Identity (CoI) and Chain-of-Condition (CoC) auditability down to the individual patient vein-to-vein loop:

  • Patented Veri-Clean® Decontamination: Standard liquid nitrogen dewars use hydrophobic porous foam to absorb LN2; biological contaminants from prior shipments can lodge in the foam. Cryoport's proprietary Veri-Clean® process achieves a validated >6-log (99.9999%) reduction of bacteria, fungi, viruses, and mycoplasma between every single trip.
  • Cryoportal® Software Integration: Hospital apheresis nurses and CDMO QA/QC directors release commercial CAR-T batches only after Cryoportal® confirms zero temperature excursions or tilt shocks during transit.
  • Inelastic Cost Share: A complete round-trip Cryoport Express® logistics package costs $2,500 to $4,500—less than 0.5% to 0.9% of a $450,000–$500,000 CAR-T therapy (and ~0.15% of a $2.2M–$3.5M gene therapy). No commercial biopharma sponsor will risk a $500,000 batch rejection or an FDA CMC amendment to save $500 on a secondary shipper.
