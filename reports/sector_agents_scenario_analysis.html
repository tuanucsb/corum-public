2. In-Depth Scenario Deconstructions

Scenario A: Universal & Strategic Tariff Escalation

Macro Inputs: TARIFFS: +1.5, INFLATION: +0.6, GROWTH: -0.4, CHINA_GROWTH: -0.8

  • The Historical Trap: In 2018-2019, US retail and industrial assemblers absorbed tariffs with manageable margin erosion because consumer balance sheets were liquid and inflation was sub-2%.
  • The Stage-Evolution Reality Today: After 3 years of 20%+ cumulative post-pandemic inflation, lower/middle-tier consumer balance sheets are exhausted with credit card delinquencies at 15-year highs. Retailers and consumer hardware assemblers have zero margin absorption cushion.
  • Winners & Resilient Subsectors: Domestic Defense contractors, Merchant Nuclear power, Domestic Steel & Critical Minerals (mandated domestic sourcing subsidies), Utilities.
  • Vulnerable Subsectors: Consumer Discretionary & Retail, Auto assemblers reliant on Mexican/Asian supply chains, Technology Hardware assemblers, China-exposed Semis.

Scenario B: COVID-19 Style Exogenous Shock

Macro Inputs: GROWTH: -1.5, FED_RATE: -1.2, OIL_PRICE: -1.4, AI_DEMAND: +0.8

  • The Historical Trap: In March 2020, asset-light tech and cloud software surged +100% because capital expenditure was minimal and zero-bound interest rates expanded multiples infinitely.
  • The Stage-Evolution Reality Today: Technology is no longer an asset-light software business. Technology today is a heavy industrial capex industry with $700B+ annual hyperscaler commitments in physical gigawatt substations, nuclear power plants, and specialized data centers. A freeze in global mobility and aggregate growth would stress corporate debt refinancing for AI debt.
  • Winners: Cybersecurity, Mission-Critical Cloud Infrastructure, Telecommunications, Managed Healthcare.
  • Vulnerable: Commercial Real Estate, Airlines & Aerospace, Traditional Energy (physical storage exhaustion), Discretionary Retail.

Scenario C: Macro Financial Shock (Stagflation & Liquidity Crunch)

Macro Inputs: OIL_PRICE: +1.5, INFLATION: +1.2, FED_RATE: +0.8, GROWTH: -0.7

  • Transmission Mechanism: Energy price spike ($120/bbl Hormuz disruption) forces central banks into hawkish defensiveness, spiking discount rates while real economic growth slows.
  • Most Fragile Archetype: High financial leverage + low pricing power (Regional Banks, Non-Datacenter REITs, Leveraged Clean Tech, Subprime Consumer Credit).
  • Hedge Positioning: Long Integrated Oil & Gas, Merchant Power, Defensive Gold / Critical Materials; short high-duration unhedged real estate and speculative unprofitable tech.

Scenario D: AI Supercycle Run-Up & Acceleration

Macro Inputs: AI_DEMAND: +1.5, FED_RATE: +0.2, GROWTH: +0.3

  • The Stage-Evolution Reality: The subsectors that benefited in Phase 1 (GPUs) are now meeting physical bottleneck realities. The highest operational torque is shifting to Power & Grid Infrastructure (Transformers, Switchgear, Merchant Nuclear, Datacenter Colocation, Cooling Systems).
  • Vulnerability Alert: Hardware semis in PEAK_RISK_MANIA stage trade at extreme multiples where even a 5% capex guidance pause triggers severe multiple contraction.
