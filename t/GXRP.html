Grayscale XRP Trust (XRP)

GXRP aims to track the spot price of XRP, less expenses and liabilities. It intends to provide accessibility to XRP without the complexities of acquiring, holding, and trading directly through an XRP spot market. GXRP owns and passively holds actual XRP through their custodian.
